The original arcade HTML content and original arcade icon were supplied by the project owner. Their ownership and any pre-existing rights are unchanged.

The Android wrapper, dynamic arcade menu, metadata files, pictographic catalog icons, Neon Bowling game, Music Maker code, Music Maker samples, and Music Maker backing loops were created specifically for this project.
