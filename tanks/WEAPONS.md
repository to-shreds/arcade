# Tank weapon catalogue

84 weapons use 14 simulation mechanisms. Every weapon has a stable saved-game ID,
a readable description, a color, and a procedural icon variant. Each mechanism's
six weapons differ in gameplay parameters; there are no mechanically identical
recolored entries.

Damage is a nominal total budget per shot before distance falloff. Multiple
projectiles or pulses share that budget. Hitting the other tank adds points to
the shooter; hitting your own tank adds points to the other player. Scores never
decrease. Dimensions are world
pixels; spread is degrees. Terrain excavation is multiplied by craterScale.
The distance falloff exponent controls whether damage is concentrated at the
center or spread more gently across a blast.

Mines retain their owner and count down once per subsequent shot. Nearby tanks
can trigger them immediately, including by moving. Any mines still present at
the end of the twentieth shot detonate before the final score is decided.
The terrain is a height map, so underground tunnels appear as collapsed surface
channels. Tanks settle onto changed terrain after each impact.

Icons and impact graphics are generated by the renderer from the mechanism,
variant, color, size, and count, so all artwork works offline.
