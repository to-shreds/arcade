    A quick railway lesson

    1. Pick an engine. Its card shows its next station and cargo.
    2. Point the junction arrows. Tap a numbered junction, then choose its next track. The dashed line previews your selected engine’s route.
    3. Send the engine. It follows the arrows. At a station, a red signal, or points facing backward, it stops.
    4. Mind the other trains. A signal holds arrivals. Brake stops an engine at its next junction. A long rescued wagon makes its engine slower.
    5. Collect, repair, deliver. A wagon couples automatically. Repairs take three seconds, then you send the engine onward.

    Pause and slow speed are always available. Finish for one star, finish without a route hint for another, and beat the timetable for a third. Timetable stars reward running safe routes together. You can retry as often as you like.
